- Verified: 2026-10-23

## Findings reported by the source

Estimated repetitions in reserve had a negligible relationship with strength gain in the best-fitting models. Muscle-size change tended to be greater when sets ended closer to failure. The authors explicitly note modest overall model fit and that repetitions in reserve were estimated from study descriptions rather than measured uniformly.

## Practical interpretation

Proximity to failure may matter more for hypertrophy than for strength, but this synthesis does not establish that every set must reach failure. Training effort can be adjusted alongside load, volume, technique, and fatigue.

## Limitations and uncertainty

The analysis is exploratory and its RIR estimates are uncertain. It cannot identify a universal optimal RIR or show that failure training is required or risk-free.
