## Framework described by NASM

NASM presents five adaptable phases: stabilization endurance, strength endurance, muscular development/hypertrophy, maximal strength, and power. Its Phase 3 example uses 3–6 sets of 6–12 repetitions at roughly 75–85% of one-repetition maximum. Its Phase 4 example emphasizes heavier loads and fewer repetitions. These numbers describe NASM's instructional system, not independently demonstrated universal physiological optima.

## Practical interpretation

The phase names can organize a trainer's program discussions and clarify the goal of a block. A client does not need to complete this exact sequence to gain muscle or strength; programming should reflect goals, experience, and tolerance.

## Limitations and uncertainty

The NASM page is professional guidance, not a systematic review comparing OPT against other systems. Its efficacy and specific rep ranges cannot be treated as established superiority. Independent load reviews in this collection report hypertrophy across more than one loading range.
