# Kassiano et al. 2023 range-of-motion review

- Source ID: kassiano_2023_rom
- Title: Which ROMs Lead to Rome? A Systematic Review of the Effects of Range of Motion on Muscle Hypertrophy
- Authors/organization: Witalo Kassiano et al.
- Publication year: 2023
- Original URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36662126/
- DOI: 10.1519/JSC.0000000000004415; PMID: 36662126
- Source type: systematic review
- Topic: full and partial exercise range of motion
- Studied population: participants in eleven included range-of-motion studies; the abstract does not define a single demographic sample.
- Verified: 2026-10-23

## Findings reported by the source

Full range and some partial ranges that trained a muscle at longer lengths often produced more growth at several measured sites than partial ranges near shorter muscle lengths. The direction varied by muscle and measurement site; one middle-range comparison favored partial-range triceps training.

## Practical interpretation

Range of motion and the muscle length emphasized by an exercise deserve attention. This review can inform exercise selection, but it does not establish that every lift must use an identical full range or that one partial range is universally superior.

## Limitations and uncertainty

The review included only eleven studies, with different muscles, exercises, and measurement sites. Its site-specific results cannot be generalized to all movements or to rehabilitation decisions.
