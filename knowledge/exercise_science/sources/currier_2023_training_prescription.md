# Currier et al. 2023 training prescription network meta-analysis

- Source ID: currier_2023_prescription
- Title: Resistance training prescription for muscle strength and hypertrophy in healthy adults: a systematic review and Bayesian network meta-analysis
- Authors/organization: Brad S Currier et al.
- Publication year: 2023
- Original URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37414459/
- DOI: 10.1136/bjsports-2023-106807; PMID: 37414459
- Source type: systematic review and Bayesian network meta-analysis
- Topic: load, sets, and frequency for strength and hypertrophy
- Studied population: healthy adults in randomized trials; 178 studies informed the strength network and 119 the hypertrophy network.
- Verified: 2026-10-23

## Findings reported by the source

All compared resistance-training prescriptions improved strength and hypertrophy relative to non-exercise controls. Prescriptions using loads above 80% of one-repetition maximum ranked highest for strength. Several load prescriptions produced comparable hypertrophy estimates, while multiple-set prescriptions ranked favorably for hypertrophy. A top rank among the compared combinations is not proof that one schedule is uniquely best for every person.

## Practical interpretation

For maximal strength, include practice with heavier loads when appropriate. For muscle growth, a range of loads can be considered, with enough sets and a sustainable schedule. Preference and adherence may matter when estimated outcomes are similar.

## Limitations and uncertainty

Network comparisons include indirect evidence and preset prescription categories. The analyses concern healthy adults, not clinical rehabilitation. Ranking probabilities and group averages do not establish individual optima.
